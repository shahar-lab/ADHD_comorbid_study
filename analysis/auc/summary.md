# Analysis Notebook: ROC/AUC Discrimination Analysis (ADHD vs. TD)

**Analysis type:** ROC/AUC (frequentist), via `pROC` — not a Bayesian regression.
**Date Created:** 2026-08-29

## 1. Hypotheses
* How well does each questionnaire's raw score discriminate participants with ADHD from typically developing (TD) participants?
* This is evaluated separately for each of 10 questionnaires: AQ, ASRS, WURS, BDI, OCI-R, PQ-B, STAI-State, STAI-Trait, PATAS, ICAR.

## 2. Variables
* **Outcome (binary class):** `group_declared` (`ADHD` / `TD`). ADHD is the positive/case class, TD is the negative/control class (`levels = c("TD", "ADHD")` in every `pROC::roc()` call).
* **Predictor:** the raw questionnaire score, one questionnaire per ROC curve — `aq`, `asrs`, `wurs`, `bdi`, `ocir`, `pqb`, `stai_state`, `stai_trait`, `patas_sum` (PATAS), `icar`.
* Computed directly from individual-level raw scores and group labels — does **not** use posterior medians, `pd`, or mean ratios from the sibling `analysis/*_regression_analysis/` Bayesian analyses in this repo.

## 3. Direction
* For the 9 symptom/psychopathology questionnaires (AQ, ASRS, WURS, BDI, OCI-R, PQ-B, STAI-State, STAI-Trait, PATAS), direction is fixed so a higher raw score counts toward ADHD, matching their known scoring convention.
* ICAR (a cognitive-ability measure with no a priori symptom direction toward ADHD) uses `direction = "auto"`; the direction pROC actually found is recorded in `output/roc_sample_sizes.csv` (`Direction_used` / `Direction_meaning`) and referenced in the results paragraph.

## 4. Sample
* Source: `data/processed_data/df_remove_diagnosis_contradiction.Rdata` (canonical processed dataframe, per this repo's CLAUDE.md).
* `group_declared` already contains only `ADHD`/`TD` in this file — no extra filtering needed.
* Each questionnaire's ROC is fit on its own complete-case subset (non-missing `group_declared` and non-missing score on that questionnaire); N therefore differs across questionnaires and is reported per questionnaire in `output/roc_summary_table.csv` and `output/roc_sample_sizes.csv`. No imputation. No questionnaire was excluded outright — all 10 are reported, including PATAS despite its substantially smaller N (~194 of 465 in past analyses).

## 5. Cutoffs
* **Optimal ROC cutoff:** selected per questionnaire via Youden's J statistic on this sample's own ROC curve (`pROC::coords(..., best.method = "youden")`). Data-derived, sample-specific.
* **Literature-based cutoff:** reported separately, for the 7 questionnaires with a previously established clinical threshold already used elsewhere in this repo — ASRS ≥ 40, WURS ≥ 36, BDI ≥ 14, OCI-R ≥ 21, PQ-B ≥ 7, STAI-State ≥ 40, STAI-Trait ≥ 44 (STAI-Trait: 44 was chosen over the 40 used in `analysis/trial_analysis` for a different severity-banding purpose — confirmed with the user). AQ, PATAS, and ICAR have no established literature-based cutoff and are left blank in that column.
* See `output/cutoff_methodology_note.md` for the full explanation of how these two cutoffs differ and should not be conflated.

## 6. Statistics reported per questionnaire
* AUC, 95% CI (DeLong, `pROC::ci.auc()`), p-value vs. AUC = .50 (one-sample z-test on the DeLong SE), Youden-optimal cutoff, sensitivity, specificity at that cutoff (`output/roc_summary_table.csv`); N per group, PPV/NPV at the Youden cutoff, and the direction used (`output/roc_sample_sizes.csv`, supplementary — the exact main-table column list in the approved spec has one `N` column and no PPV/NPV/direction columns).
* PPV/NPV are computed at this case-control sample's own prevalence (pROC default) and are flagged in `output/cutoff_methodology_note.md` as not generalizable diagnostic probabilities.

## 7. Cross-questionnaire comparison
* Pairwise AUC comparisons (all 45 pairs) via DeLong's test, paired, each pair refit on the common-case subsample of participants with non-missing data on **both** questionnaires in that pair (`output/pairwise_auc_comparisons.csv`) — a naive full-sample paired test would silently fall back to an unpaired test given each questionnaire's differing missingness, which the spec explicitly ruled out.
* Holm-corrected across all 45 pairs; both raw and adjusted p-values reported.

## 8. Figures
* One ROC plot per questionnaire (`output/roc_<questionnaire>.pdf`/`.png`, 10 total): 1 − specificity (x) vs. sensitivity (y), 0–1 axes on both (`coord_equal`), dashed chance diagonal, AUC + 95% CI annotated, Youden cutoff point marked.
* One combined overlay plot (`output/roc_combined.pdf`/`.png`) with all 10 curves color-coded (colorblind-safe palette, `COLOR_STANDARD.md`) and a legend giving each questionnaire's AUC.
