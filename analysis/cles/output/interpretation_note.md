## Interpretation note

The Common Language Odds of Superiority is scale-free and accounts for each group's within-group variance (via the heteroskedastic model's per-group sigma), not just the raw mean difference, so it allows a direct comparison of discrimination strength across questionnaires that use different raw-score ranges. As with the accompanying CLES, Cohen's d, and clinical-cutoff risk ratios, it describes the size of the ADHD-vs-TD group difference observed in this sample and is not a causal or diagnostic claim.
